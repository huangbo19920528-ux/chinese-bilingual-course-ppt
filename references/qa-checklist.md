# Whole-deck QA Checklist

## Content and textbook fidelity

- [ ] Lesson title, unit, outcomes, sequence, and key terms agree with the textbook and teaching schedule.
- [ ] Enrichment material is clearly marked.
- [ ] English terminology, capitalization, grammar, symbols, and units are correct.
- [ ] Large textbook screenshots have been converted to editable text where appropriate.
- [ ] The final takeaway is understandable without additional guessing.

## Bilingual support

- [ ] Most knowledge-bearing pages contain both English and Chinese.
- [ ] Chinese explains meaning or logic instead of mechanically duplicating English.
- [ ] Core terms are introduced before dense reading.
- [ ] Student tasks are unambiguous in both content and expected output.

## Projection and layout

- [ ] Titles are normally blue; red is used only for deliberate warning or contrast.
- [ ] Main body text is normally at least 24 px.
- [ ] Title-to-body gaps are compact and consistent.
- [ ] Sparse pages use larger type and line spacing.
- [ ] Dense pages have been rewritten, resized, or split without illegible shrinkage.
- [ ] No overlap, overflow, clipping, or accidental image occlusion.
- [ ] Font sizes, line spacing, font weights, margins, and capitalization feel consistent.
- [ ] No unnecessary first-line indentation.
- [ ] The page is informative and comfortably full when projected.

## Images

- [ ] Images are relevant, authentic, sharp enough, and properly cropped.
- [ ] Images do not cover teaching text.
- [ ] Visuals are distributed through the lesson.
- [ ] Each externally sourced image has provenance in notes.
- [ ] Decorative images have meaningful alt text or are intentionally marked decorative.

## Video

- [ ] The lesson contains about 8–10 separate candidate video pages.
- [ ] Each candidate is normally no longer than eight minutes, or a usable excerpt is specified.
- [ ] Every URL is clickable and traceable.
- [ ] Each page has preview terms and Before/During/After tasks.
- [ ] Candidates are distributed near relevant concepts.
- [ ] Availability, subtitles, and download status are not overstated.

## Speaker notes and sources

- [ ] Every slide has a usable talk track.
- [ ] Every slide has a classroom operation or transition.
- [ ] Timing is included.
- [ ] Difficult mechanisms or final summaries include board/emphasis guidance.
- [ ] Every slide has a `[Sources]` block.
- [ ] Textbook, video, image, data, and external claims are traceable.

## Technical verification

- [ ] All slide edits use the accepted deck as the template.
- [ ] Theme, master layouts, slide order, media, hyperlinks, and notes are preserved.
- [ ] Every slide is rendered after the final edit.
- [ ] Every changed slide is inspected at full size.
- [ ] The whole-deck montage is reviewed for rhythm and outliers.
- [ ] The PPTX package test passes.
- [ ] The final deck is editable, not a collection of full-slide images.
